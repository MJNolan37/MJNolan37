# Matthew Nolan

Portfolio Evaluation Analyst with a background in trading, financial analysis, and strategic project design — currently focused on quantitative research in market regime dynamics, volatility, and dispersion.

## Research Philosophy

I build things to find out where they break. A regime model, a signal, a forecasting tool — the interesting question is never "does this look good," it's "what happens when I try to prove it wrong." The [V&D Cloud](https://github.com/Telos-in-the-Void/vd-cloud) research below is the clearest example: a coherent diagnostic framework that was pushed through baseline challenges, autocorrelation-robust inference, permutation testing, and a genuine out-of-sample holdout — and reported honestly when it didn't clear the bar for a trading signal.

## Featured Research

### [V&D Cloud — Volatility & Dispersion Regime Research](https://github.com/Telos-in-the-Void/vd-cloud)
A clean-room research project testing whether volatility and cross-sectional dispersion can support an Ichimoku-inspired market-regime framework, across QQQ and a developed-market ETF surface. Validated with HAC/Newey-West inference, random-label permutation testing, baseline challenges, and a chronological out-of-sample holdout. Includes **Atlas Observatory**, an interactive live monitoring dashboard extending the research into day-to-day instrumentation.
