# Matthew Nolan

I build Python research tools for market analysis, from data preparation and statistical checks to interactive dashboards. My projects are also a way to learn: build the method, challenge the result, and explain what the evidence supports.

## Selected Work

### [V&D Cloud and Atlas](https://github.com/MJNolan37/vd-cloud)
Built a volatility-and-dispersion research pipeline for QQQ and developed-market ETFs, compared its regime framework with simpler baselines, and developed Atlas as a browser-based monitoring dashboard. The revised analysis did not establish predictive validity or a consistent advantage over the baselines; Atlas carries forward the descriptive measures.

### [Project Parallax](https://github.com/MJNolan37/Project-Parallax)
Rebuilt a published market / industry / firm equity variance decomposition on 2010–2024 public data, with 24 known-answer validation cases. Phase 1 is complete. The next empirical stage remained gated at the [October 25, 2026 snapshot](https://github.com/MJNolan37/Project-Parallax/blob/38411cc050696efde85d7702c9a11b9ad985c693/docs/A1_STATUS.md), including an unresolved statistical threshold.

### [Options Positioning Research](https://github.com/MJNolan37/options-positioning-research)
Built a synthetic option-chain testbed with a documented schema, contract normalization, eight positioning and liquidity features, automated checks, and descriptive charts across 65 dates. It demonstrates data engineering and research preparation; the synthetic options data do not establish predictive or trading performance.
